## Sadr Region — IC 1318 and the Crescent Nebula

IC 1318 is a large emission nebula surrounding Sadr (γ Cygni), the central star of the Northern Cross in Cygnus. The wide field reveals an intricate web of glowing hydrogen gas shaped by stellar winds and ultraviolet radiation from nearby hot stars. Within this region, the Crescent Nebula (NGC 6888) stands out as a tighter shell of gas energized by a Wolf-Rayet star — one of the most luminous and short-lived types of star known.
